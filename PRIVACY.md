# Privacy Policy — DataCanvas

*Last updated: October 1, 2026*

DataCanvas by Ranuk IT Solutions (Emilio Ranucoli) respects your privacy.

## 1. Local Processing
DataCanvas processes data locally in the client browser using WebAssembly (DuckDB-WASM). Raw tabular data, CSVs, and spreadsheets uploaded to ChatGPT conversation panels are processed in the user's browser sandbox and are not stored or sold by Ranuk IT Solutions.

## 2. Payments
External payments for DataCanvas Pro are processed securely by Stripe. We do not collect or store credit card details.

## 3. Contact & Support
For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at: soporte@ranuk.dev or open an issue on GitHub.

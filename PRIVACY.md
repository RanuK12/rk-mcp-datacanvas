# Privacy Policy — DataCanvas

*Last updated: October 14, 2026*

DataCanvas by Ranuk IT Solutions (Emilio Ranucoli) respects your privacy.

## 1. How your data is processed
DataCanvas does not process your data only in your browser. The tabular data you paste or upload in ChatGPT (CSV, TSV, JSON) is sent to the DataCanvas MCP server, which profiles the columns and builds the dashboard shown in the side panel. When you export a PDF report, the server generates that file. Ranuk IT Solutions does not sell your data.

## 2. Payments
DataCanvas Pro is not available yet. When it is, payments will be processed by Stripe; we do not collect or store credit card details.

## 3. Contact & Support
For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at: soporte@ranuk.dev or open an issue on GitHub.
